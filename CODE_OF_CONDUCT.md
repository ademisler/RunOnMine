# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful,
assume good intent, discuss technical decisions with evidence, and avoid
publishing another person's private information or credentials.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sustained
disruption, and disclosure of private or security-sensitive information.
Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these rules.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
